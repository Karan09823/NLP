fuzzy_ratio -> order of sequence matter for comparing

fuzzy_partial_ratio -> Does shorter string matches with portion of longer string

token_sort_ratio -> works on the principle of fuzzy_ratio with a set of process included like tokenize,sort token, join again,calculate normal fuzzy_ratio 
(under the hood process)

token_set_ratio -> works on unique words shared between the strings
